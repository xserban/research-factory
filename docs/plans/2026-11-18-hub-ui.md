## 1. Goal and scope

**Goal:**
- Replace the cluttered hub with a calm, consistent, inspiring interface.
- Every screen answers one question with a computed lead sentence.
- Every figure leads to its rows and shows how it was computed.
- The corpus can be explored in several innovative ways that each answer a stated question.

**In scope:**
- One design system: tokens, type, controls, the shell with a rail, Ctrl-K and the paper drawer.
- Four pages:
  - Home.
  - Flow: the Sankey plus the waffle with every record.
  - Lenses: design space, design chains, citation constellation, field over time, taxonomy icicle, evidence wall, citation beeswarm.
  - Explore papers: sentence query, rings, semantic zoom, paper DNA with the compare tray, and the trail.
- The data payloads behind them.
- The reconciliation of corpus sets and counts.
- Cut-over from the old shell.

**Out of scope here:**
- Speed of product builds (IDEA-96).
- Redesign of the other 19 product pages. They stay reachable through Ctrl-K ("Classic" pages) and are restyled later, page by page, under the same kit.
- Authentication.
- Any model call: the hub stays a projection, and "similar" is a stated formula over recorded fields.

## 2. What must hold (rigour)

1. **Numbers are computed, never typed (R-05, CLAUDE.md).**
   - Every count on every page comes from a service payload built from events.
   - The page never hard-codes a number.
   - A test pins each lead sentence to its payload.
2. **Named sets, never conflated.**
   - "Included" means the PRISMA final included set (`prisma_flow`).
   - "Mined" means works with a live pass-A miner row (`corpus_payload` scope).
   - The payload carries both sets, their difference by work id, and the reason for each difference.
   - The prototypes found 370 mined against 365 included, unexplained; the build explains it.
3. **One citation count.**
   - `cited_in_corpus` sums to 817, but there are 809 resolved links in the pilot.
   - The build finds the cause and either reconciles the two or shows both under different names.
4. **Provenance.** Every classified value shown carries its verbatim quote and anchor, as `extraction_fields` records them.
5. **Held-out rule (decision 37.9).** Held-out seeds' works are never hidden or masked in any view.
6. **Accessibility.**
   - WCAG AA is checked on the token pairs in both themes (a computed test, as `test_site_themes.py` does).
   - Visible focus.
   - Every chart has a keyboard-reachable list alternative.
   - Reduced motion keeps the final state.
7. **Offline.**
   - No external resource at run time.
   - Fonts (Newsreader, Inter, JetBrains Mono, all OFL) are vendored as woff2 with their licence files.
8. **The platform never calls a model.** Similarity, ranking and "largest gaps" are deterministic formulas, stated on screen.

## 3. Architecture

- **Data, not inlined HTML.**
  - Each page is a static HTML file, the same for every project, loading shared `kit.css` and `shell.js`, its own page script, and one or two JSON payloads.
  - Payloads are built by services and cached on disk like products, keyed by the same fingerprint.
  - Served with `ETag` and `Cache-Control: private, max-age=0, must-revalidate`, so browsers reuse unchanged payloads. The current hub sends `no-store` for everything.
- **New modules (services own the logic):**

  | Module | Holds |
  |---|---|
  | `services/hub_payloads.py` | Payload builders: `home_payload`, `flow_payload`, `corpus_payload_v2` (works, fields with quotes, cites, sets), `sets_reconciliation`. They reuse `prisma_flow`, `flow_works`, `citation_report` and the `corpus_payload` queries. Pure functions of a `Project`. |
  | `services/hub_lenses.py` | Lens computations that need care server-side: the matrix with expected counts under independence, chains (top-k per column plus "Rest"), the beeswarm citation shares, and the similarity score. The browser draws; the service computes and tests. |
  | `services/hub_ui.py` | The asset registry (page names, file paths, content types) and the HTML page template (title, as-of, project slug). No business logic. |

- **Static assets:** a package data folder `factory/hub_ui/`.
  - `kit.css`, `shell.js`, `home.js`, `flow.js`, `lenses.js`, `explore.js`, `pages/*.html`, `fonts/*.woff2`, `fonts/LICENSES/`.
  - Ported from the round-2 prototypes. The vendored fonts replace the `@import` from Google.
- **Routes (thin, in the server):**
  - `/p/<slug>/ui/` for pages.
  - `/p/<slug>/ui/assets/<name>`, served immutable, with a version in the name.
  - `/p/<slug>/ui/data/<payload>.json`.
  - The old hub stays at `/p/<slug>/` until cut-over.
- **The shell links out:**
  - The 19 classic products open by Ctrl-K or rail groups as full pages, not in an iframe.
  - The paper drawer is shared.
  - The theme follows one token family (section 5, ruling R2).

## 6. Risks

- **JS size and quality.** The prototypes are about 4k lines of hand-written JS by different agents.
  - Mitigation: in porting, factor the shared pieces into `shell.js` (works list, chips, tooltip, hash state); each page script stays under ~800 lines; no library.
  - The JS has no type checker. Mitigation: `// @ts-check` with JSDoc types and `tsc --noEmit` only if node is present (optional, non-gating).
- **Untested interactions.**
  - The prototypes never exercised hover, canvas hit-testing or the `P` key on Explore.
  - Mitigation: the browser smoke (step 8) drives clicks through the Chrome DevTools protocol for the main paths. The rest is part of the live check.
- **Collision with IDEA-96.** Steps 1–8 touch no file the speed line changes. Step 9 is done after its merge, on a fresh rebase.
- **Two citation counts and two sets.** If the cause is a defect in an existing view, the fix belongs to that view, not to the hub. It needs its own test and a note in the handoff.
- **390 px.** The wide charts scroll inside their card. That is accepted for v2, with a stacked list as the narrow-width alternative.
